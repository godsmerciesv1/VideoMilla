How it works

You approve every picture before a single clip is made, and you see the price before anything is spent.

Write or draw

Type a sentence, paste a script, or add a drawing. The AI can draft the script for you to review first.

Made for storytelling

A voice for every character

Each character has its own voice, and the narrator is separate. The voice never changes halfway through a video.

You decide what moves

The AI recommends about one shot in three to animate. Move the slider to choose more and see the price first.

Series with a locked cast

Keep your characters, places and a running summary, then make the next episode with the same cast.

Captions that read right

Burned-in captions in three styles, shaped correctly for every script, including right-to-left languages.
