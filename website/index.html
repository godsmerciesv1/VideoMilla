Turn a sentence, a script or a drawing into a narrated, captioned video made of shots.

Open the web app

A few shots are animated; the rest are pictures with a slow pan or zoom. That is how the videos are made, and the samples show exactly that mix.

How it works

1

Write or draw

Type a sentence, paste a script, or add a drawing. The AI can draft the script for you to review first.

2

Pick a look and a cast

Choose one of eight styles, build your characters and give each one a voice.

3

Approve the pictures

Every shot starts as a picture you approve. You choose which shots to animate and see the price first.

4

Render and share

The finished MP4 has narration and burned-in captions. Save it or share it from your phone or browser.
