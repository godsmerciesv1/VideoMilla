Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026. This policy covers the VideoMilla Android app, the VideoMilla web app and this website. English is the governing language.

Who we are

The app and service are called VideoMilla. For any privacy question, or to exercise a right, contact support.

What is processed

  • Scripts, ideas and prompts you submit, to write and structure your video.
  • Reference pictures you add, to check them and to guide the look of your pictures. They are checked for human faces and are not stored on our servers.
  • Cartoon Me photo (when this option is available): one photo of your own face, processed transiently to make a cartoon character. It is not stored by us and is deleted from your device after use.
  • Line audio for talking shots, sent to the video provider so the character's mouth matches the voice.
  • Purchase data: which pack was bought, through which store, and a one-way hash of the purchase reference.
  • Reports you send about an output.

What we do not do

  • No ads and no tracking of you across other apps or sites.
  • We do not store your pictures or your scripts on our servers. Projects and finished videos stay on your device or in your browser.
  • The only personal data kept about you is the email address you sign in with (or your Google account identifier) and your wallet records.

Services that process data for us

  • Cloudflare: hosting, the API, the database, short-term clip storage, AI models for scripts, pictures, voices and safety checks, and login email delivery.
  • Google Cloud: text-to-speech voices.
  • Segmind and Pruna: animated clip generation.
  • Google (Gemini): a backup provider for scripts and clips if the main ones are unavailable.
  • Brevo: login email delivery.
  • Google: Sign in with Google, and Google Play for Android purchases.
  • Polar: seller of record for web purchases. It receives the email of the account you are signed in with so that checkout opens pre-filled.

Age

VideoMilla is for people aged 13 and over. It is not a children's app.

Your rights

You can export your server data and delete it at any time in the app (Settings), or by email from your account address or with your Support ID. See Delete your account. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to access, correct or restrict processing of your data; write to the contact above.

Transfers

Our providers operate internationally, so your data may be processed in countries other than your own.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
