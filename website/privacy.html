Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026. This policy covers the VideoMilla Android app, web app and website.

Who we are

VideoMilla is the app, web app and website this policy covers. If you have a privacy question or want to use a right described below, contact support.

What we collect

  • What you give us to make a video: scripts, ideas, prompts, pictures you add and audio you choose. It is used only to make your video.
  • Your account: the email address you sign in with, or your Google account identifier if you use Sign in with Google, and an identifier for each device you use.
  • Credits and purchases: your balance, your purchase history and purchase references. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Reports and messages you send us.
  • Basic technical information such as the app version, needed to run the service and keep it secure.

How we use it

To make your videos, run your account and wallet, process purchases, keep the service safe and prevent abuse, answer support requests, and meet our legal obligations.

What we do not do

  • No ads, and no tracking of you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not keep your scripts and pictures on our servers. Your projects and finished videos stay on your device or in your browser.

Temporary processing

To generate your content we process what you submit for a short time and then discard it. Some generated files, such as animated clips, are held briefly so your device can download them, and are deleted shortly afterwards, normally within a day. If you use Cartoon Me, your photo is processed only to make the cartoon character and is not stored; it is also removed from your device afterwards.

Service providers

We use trusted service providers to run VideoMilla, for example for hosting, AI processing, voice generation, sign-in, email delivery and payments. They handle data only to provide their service to us, under their own terms and privacy commitments. Purchases are processed by the app store or payment provider you use, and their privacy policies apply to that part. Providers can change as we improve the service.

How long we keep it

We keep account and wallet information while your account exists. When you delete your account we delete it, except for purchase records we may need to keep, which are anonymized.

Your rights and choices

You can export your data and delete your account in the app (Settings). You can also contact support. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. See Delete your account.

Age

VideoMilla is for people aged 13 and over. It is not a children's app.

Security and transfers

We use reasonable measures to protect your data, though no system is perfectly secure. Our providers operate internationally, so your data may be processed in countries other than your own.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
