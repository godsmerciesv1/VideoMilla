Acceptable use and content rules

  • sexual content, or any sexual or romantic content involving a person who is or appears to be under 18, in any style;

Accounts and the shared wallet

You can sign in with your email address or with Google. You need to be signed in to buy credits and to use Cartoon Me. Each account has one wallet shared by the Android app and the web app. Up to 10 signed-in devices can use one account. Keep access to your email address private: whoever can sign in to the account can spend its credits. Projects and finished videos stay on the device or browser that made them.

Credits and purchases

  • Credits are used only inside VideoMilla and never expire.
  • What an action costs in credits can change for future spending. The price is always shown before you confirm.
  • All purchases are final. See Refunds for what happens if a store or payment provider reverses a purchase.
  • On Android, purchases are made through Google Play. On the web, Polar is the seller of record and handles payment.
  • A technical failure (credits paid for but not delivered) is fixed by re-crediting.

Cartoon Me

When available, Cartoon Me lets you turn a photo of your own face into a cartoon character. You confirm that the face is your own and that you are 18 or older. The photo must show exactly one face and no one who appears to be under 18. It works only in the four cartoon styles and is limited to 3 per account in any 30 days.

Referrals

Every account has a referral code. When a new account uses your code and makes a first purchase of a pack of at least 90 credits, the new account receives 10% of that pack in extra credits immediately, and you receive the same amount after 14 days. Limits apply (20 qualifying referrals and 500 bonus credits in any 30 days). If the qualifying purchase is refunded or reversed within those 14 days, both bonuses are removed. Referring yourself is not allowed. Bonus credits are ordinary credits.
