Support

Write to stmakarios@gmail.com. Please include your Support ID if the question is about credits or a purchase.

Your Support ID

The Support ID is an 8-character code that helps us find your wallet. In the app, open Settings and then Wallet. It is not secret and it is not a password; it cannot be used to sign in.

Signing in

  • Email: enter your address and we send a message with a button and a 6-digit code. On the web, tap the button. On Android, type the code into the app. The code and link last 15 minutes and work once.
  • No email? Check your spam folder, wait a minute and request a new one. You can also sign in with Google.
  • New phone? Sign in with the same account and your credits are there. Projects and videos stay on the device that made them unless you exported them.

Report content

Every output in the app has a Report control. You can also email us with the details. Reports are reviewed within 72 hours as a target.

Frequently asked questions

Does VideoMilla have a free trial?

No. The sample videos show what it makes. Credits never expire, so you can start with a small pack.

Why are only some shots animated?

Animated clips cost the most to make. The app recommends about one shot in three and the rest are pictures with a slow pan or zoom. You can choose more and see the price first.

Can I get a refund?

All purchases are final. See Refunds. If credits were paid for but not delivered, write to us and we will re-credit them.

My video did not render.

Rendering needs free storage on your device. If it fails, the app offers to export your approved pictures and audio instead. In the web app, use a current Chrome or Edge, and keep the tab open while it renders.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open Settings and choose to delete your server data. See Delete your account.

I found a translation problem.

In Settings, choose Report a translation problem, or email us.
